Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

At Attrivo, data integrity, transparency, and data sovereignty compliance are central to our design. This Privacy Policy outlines exactly how our software infrastructure parses, treats, and isolates information handled on behalf of enterprise partners across global systems.

1. Log Types and Data Ingestion

To compute precise marketing performance indicators, our infrastructure processes raw interaction triggers, click identifiers, session timestamps, device parameters, platform classes, and network routing metadata. This information is analyzed explicitly to identify conversion paths and isolate fraud threats.

When administrators intentionally configure native integrations with partners like Facebook (Meta) or Google Ads, our system stores related authorization strings securely, including App IDs, OAuth tokens, and Decryption Keys. Attrivo acts strictly as a data processor. All ingested transaction events remain your exclusive property. Our processing modules are structurally isolated to align robustly with applicable local and international data protection laws, emphasizing minimized persistent profiling, restricted cross-border data leakage, and automated record-clearing timelines when requested by system admins.
