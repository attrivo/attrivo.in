FAQ

Questions teams ask before they switch

What is Attrivo?
Attrivo is an enterprise attribution and analytics platform for web and app. Marketing teams use one SDK and one dashboard to measure ROI, journeys, segments, push engagement, and fraud.
Does Attrivo keep data in India?
Yes. Attrivo is built for Indian enterprises that need measurement without sending customer data across borders.
Can we measure both web and app?
Yes. Attrivo unifies web and app clicks, installs, events, and conversions so growth teams are not stitching multiple MMPs and analytics tools.
How does pricing work?
There is a free tier up to 15,000 installs, a Growth plan at ₹5 per conversion, and custom Enterprise pricing with SLAs and warehouse delivery.
How do we get started?
Request a demo from the website or email info@attrivo.in. The team is based in Bangalore.
