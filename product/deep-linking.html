Deep Linking

Every click should land in the right experience.

Attrivo deep linking connects paid and owned channels to the exact in-app or web destination your user expects — so journeys stay contextual from first tap to conversion.

Personalized in-app destinations

Send new and returning users straight to the offer, product, or screen that matches the campaign they clicked — not a generic home screen.

Web-to-app continuity

Guide high-intent website visitors into the app with smart banners and contextual links, preserving campaign context for measurement.

Owned-channel reactivation

Route email, SMS, QR, and push traffic into specific app experiences so re-engagement campaigns convert without broken redirects.

Deferred journeys for new users

When the app is not installed yet, park the destination until after install — then open the intended screen automatically.

Cross-platform routing

One link adapts to device and OS: app store, Universal Links / App Links, or a fallback web URL when needed.

Measured end-to-end

Deep links stay tied to attribution and analytics, so marketing teams can measure installs, opens, and downstream conversions from the same journey.
