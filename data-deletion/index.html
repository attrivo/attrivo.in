User Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: August 2026

Attrivo Intelligence Private Limited ("Attrivo") provides these instructions so you can request deletion of personal data we hold about you, including data associated with Meta / Facebook integrations.

How to request deletion

  1. Email info@attrivo.in with the subject line "Data Deletion Request."
  2. Include enough information for us to locate your data — for example, your name, email address, the app or website through which your data was collected, or your Facebook/Meta user ID if your request relates to data received via a Meta integration.
  3. We will verify your request and confirm receipt within a reasonable time, then delete or irreversibly anonymize the relevant data, except where retention is required by law.

Default retention for Media Partner data

By default, Attrivo retains user-level attribution data shared with advertising and Media Partners — including Meta / Facebook — for 6 months from the date of the relevant install or event. After this period, the data is automatically deleted or irreversibly anonymized, unless a Customer has configured a different retention period or a longer period is required by law. Aggregated, anonymized data that cannot reasonably be used to identify an individual, device, or End User may be retained beyond this period.

If you use a Customer's app or website

If you are an End User of a Customer's app or website that uses Attrivo, the fastest route is usually to contact that Customer directly, since they control your account. Attrivo will honor verified requests submitted directly to us as well, and will coordinate with the relevant Customer where needed.

Contact: info@attrivo.in
